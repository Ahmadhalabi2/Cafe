Our Story

Born From a Love of Coffee

Brûlé started as a small corner spot with one espresso machine and a big dream. Today it's become a beloved gathering place for those who take their coffee seriously — and their downtime even more so.

We source directly from farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. Every bean is roasted in-house in small batches to honour its unique character.

Direct-trade beans from 12+ origins
In-house small-batch roasting
Award-winning barista team
